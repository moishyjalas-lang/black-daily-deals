Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Everyday Black Friday ("we", "us") helps you find deals from stores you trust. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect, how we use cookies, and your choices. We keep data collection to a minimum.

Affiliate disclosure

We earn commissions from some links. Everyday Black Friday is a participant in affiliate programs, including the Amazon Associates Program. When you click a deal and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes the price you pay, and we only list deals we believe are genuinely worth sharing.

Required disclosure under the U.S. FTC endorsement guidelines.

What we collect

  • If you create an account: your email address and the deal-alerts (keywords) you choose to save, so we can send you the alerts you asked for.
  • If you subscribe to the newsletter: your email address, to send you the daily deals email. You can unsubscribe anytime.
  • Basic traffic numbers: how many people visit and which pages are popular — measured in a privacy-first, cookie-free way (see below).

Cookies & similar technology

A cookie is a small file a website can store on your device. We keep ours minimal:

  • Essential — needed for the site and your sign-in to work. These always run.
  • Non-essential (analytics, marketing) — these are blocked until you choose on our cookie banner. If you pick "Essential Only," none of them load.
  • Affiliate cookies — when you click a deal out to a store (e.g. Amazon), that store may set its own cookie to credit the referral. That happens on their site, under their policy, not ours.

You can change your choice anytime from the cookie banner. We only re-ask if our cookie use materially changes.

Analytics (the privacy-first kind)

We measure visits using Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is privacy-first and does not use cookies or track you across sites. It counts page views and general traffic, not individual people.

Email

We send the newsletter and your deal-alerts through our email provider (Sender). We only use your email to send what you signed up for. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.

Who we share with

We do not sell your personal information. We share only with the service providers that run the site for us (for example, our hosting, database, email, and payment providers) and only so they can perform those services.

Your choices

  • Change your cookie choice anytime via the cookie banner.
  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email.
  • Ask us to delete your account and data by contacting us.
  • California residents: you may opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell your information.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at the email listed on our site, and we'll help.
