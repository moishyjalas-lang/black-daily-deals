Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026.

We keep this simple. We only collect what we need to run the site.

What we collect

If you sign in or sign up for the daily email, we keep your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email and profile picture with us. Your saved deals, votes and hidden stores are kept on your own device.

How we use it

To send the daily deals email and the alerts you ask for, and to show your name when you are signed in. We never sell your information.

Links to stores

When you click a deal you go to the store's website, which has its own privacy policy. Stores may use cookies to know you came from us.

Unsubscribe or delete

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To delete your information, email blackdailydeals.alerts@proton.me.
