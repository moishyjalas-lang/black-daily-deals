Black Friday Survival Guide

How to get the real deals and skip the fake ones.

1. Make a list before the sales start

Write down what you actually need and the most you will pay. Set a deal alert for each one in your account and we will email you when it drops.

2. Check the price history

Some stores raise prices in October so the "sale" looks bigger. A real deal is lower than the price over the last few months, not just lower than the "was" price.

3. Watch for the verdict

Every deal here gets a verdict. Real Deal means the lowest or near-lowest price we have seen. Good Deal means a fair discount. Meh means you can probably wait.

4. Look at the model number

Some TVs and laptops are made just for Black Friday with cheaper parts. Compare the full model number before you buy.

5. Know the return rules

Most big stores give extra time to return holiday buys. Keep your receipt email.

6. Pay smart

Use a credit card for buyer protection. Never pay a store by wire transfer or gift card.

7. Do not panic-buy

Big deals come back on Cyber Monday and in the weeks after. That is why we post deals every day, all year.
