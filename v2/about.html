About Black Daily Deals

Black Friday prices, every single day.

What we do

Black Friday is one day a year. We think good prices should be every day. Each morning we pick one Crazy Deal of the Day, list the top deals, and sort everything by category so you can find what you want fast.

How we make money

When you buy through a link on our site, the store may pay us a small fee. It costs you nothing extra. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Stores cannot pay to change a verdict. If a deal is Meh, we say so.

We never take your payment

You always buy on the store's own website. We never see your card.

Contact

Brands can send us a deal on the submit a deal page. Everyone else can reach us at blackdailydeals.alerts@proton.me.
